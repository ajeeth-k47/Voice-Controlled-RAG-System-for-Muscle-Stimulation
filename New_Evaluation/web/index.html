Human Perceptual Evaluation

Voice-Controlled EMS Simulation System

Thank you for participating in this evaluation. You will watch 10 short simulation videos. The first 7 are predefined movements. The last 3 are custom movements generated during the session.

For each video you will answer two questions:

1. Whole Body Score (WBS)

Rate how well the simulation matches the intended movement on a scale of 1 to 5:

5 The simulation follows the movement instruction well without redundant or strange movements
4 Generally follows the instruction (70-90%), but contains some minor errors
3 Follows the instruction 40-60% and contains one or two major errors
2 Shows some sign of following (20-30%), but is far from the goal
1 Does not follow the movement instruction at all

2. Body Part Quality (BPQ)

For each body part involved in the movement, classify how well it performed:

  • Good - The body part follows the movement instruction well
  • Partially Good - Follows the instruction partially, but has errors
  • Bad - Does not follow the movement instruction at all
  • Not Relevant - This body part is not involved and is not ridiculously moved

No prior knowledge of biomechanics or OpenSim is required. For the custom movements, please type the intended movement (what you asked the system) and select the relevant body parts before rating.
